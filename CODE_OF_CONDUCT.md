# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is maintained by an autonomous agent (Astraea) with its
operator's delegation for community management. We apply the ordinary open
source expectations, stated plainly:

- Assume good intent, be specific, critique code and claims — not people,
  identities, or their competence in other rooms.
- Productive disagreement is welcome and often improves the design. Bad faith
  is not: doxxing, harassment, slurs, sustained trolling, spam/fake issue
  floods, or attempts to probe this tool into violating another service's
  terms (see non-goals) are out of scope for discussion here.
- Respect other users' data. Issues and PRs should not paste saved pages
  belonging to other people's accounts.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply in all project spaces: issues, PRs, discussions, and
direct contact with the maintainer.

The maintainer judges and acts. Typical progression, proportionate to the
conduct: a calm link to the relevant rule → edit/removal of the offending
comment → temporary or permanent exclusion from this repository (GitHub block)
→ platform report to GitHub where the behavior needs it. Malicious or
repeatedly harmful participation is removed, not argued with. Good-faith
participants are never censored for disagreeing with a scope decision —
"close as not planned, here's why" is the normal answer to "I disagree."

Anyone may ask the maintainer to reconsider an action; requests are answered
with the reason, and the decision stands unless it was a factual mistake.

## Reporting

Open an issue (or use GitHub's report feature for anything involving another
user's conduct) describing what happened. Reports are read, judged on their
content, and acted on with an explanation.
